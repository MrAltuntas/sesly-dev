This Privacy Policy explains the information that Sesly ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares through our mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using our Service, you accept the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## Data Controller

The Service is developed and operated by Süleyman Yeşilyurt as an individual. Süleyman Yeşilyurt is the data controller of your personal data. You can reach us at [info@sesly.io](mailto:info@sesly.io) about anything related to your personal data.

## Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

### Information You Give Us Directly

- **Account Information:** When you sign up for our Service, we collect information such as your email address and your name. If you sign up with an email address and a password, your password is processed by our authentication provider, Firebase; we do not see or store your password. If you choose to sign up through a third-party service such as Google or Apple, we receive your name, your email address and your profile photo (if any) from that service.
- **Voice Recordings and Voice Models (Biometric Data):** When you use the voice cloning feature, we collect the voice sample you record (the "Voice Sample") and your consent recording, if any. The Voice Sample and the voice model created from it are considered special category personal data (biometric data) under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and are processed only on the basis of the explicit consent you give in the app before recording. The date on which you gave your explicit consent and the version of the text you approved are also recorded.
- **Profile Information:** You may optionally choose to add a photo to your profile.
- **Preferences:** We store your in-app preferences, such as the story language and your notification and sleep timer settings.
- **Purchase Information:** When you subscribe to a paid plan, we receive the following from Apple or Google to verify your subscription: the product you bought, the purchase or transaction identifier, the subscription period and whether the subscription has been renewed, cancelled or refunded. We do not receive your card or payment details.
- **Contact Information:** When you contact us for support requests or feedback, we collect the information you provide.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you interact with our Service. This may include which features you use, which stories you narrate, your favorites, your playlists, your listening progress, your translation requests, your plan usage counters and your in-app activity.
- **Device Information:** We collect your device's push notification identifier (push token) and your device platform (iOS or Android) to send push notifications.
- **Technical Logs:** To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and fix errors, our servers keep technical logs about incoming requests that contain information such as the IP address, the time of the request, the account identifier and, when you sign in, your email address.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Providing and Managing the Service:** To create your account, verify your identity, verify the purchases you make through the app stores, apply your plan limits and provide the core functions of our Service (such as voice cloning and story narration).
- **Voice Cloning:** We process your Voice Sample only to create an artificial copy of your voice (a voice model) and to narrate stories with this voice for your own family. We do not use your Voice Sample or your voice model for advertising or marketing purposes and we do not sell them to third parties.
- **Communication:** To send you service announcements, updates, security alerts and support messages. We send push notifications with campaign or promotional content only if you have allowed them.
- **Personalization:** To make our Service more relevant and personal for you.
- **Improvement and Analysis:** To understand how our Service is used, fix errors and develop new features.

## Sharing of Information and Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes without your consent. We share your information only in the following cases and with the following parties:

- **Service Providers:** We work with third-party companies and individuals who help us operate our Service. These providers use your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obliged not to disclose it or use it for any other purpose.
- **Authentication:** We use Firebase (Google) for user authentication services. When you sign in with Google or Apple, those platforms manage the authentication process.
- **Data Storage:** Files such as your Voice Samples, the generated story audio and your profile photos are stored on Google Cloud Storage. Your account information and usage data are stored in the database on the server that runs the Service.
- **Payment Processing:** Subscription payments are processed only by Apple In-App Purchase (Apple App Store) and Google Play Billing (Google Play Store). We do not see or store your card and payment details; they are processed directly by the relevant store. So that the purchase can be linked to your account, the app passes your Sesly user identifier to the store at the time of purchase; to verify your subscription, we receive the purchase information described above from the store.
- **Voice Cloning and Narration with a Cloned Voice:** Voice cloning and the narration of stories with your cloned voice are done with Sonic models provided by Cartesia, Inc. (United States of America). Your Voice Sample is sent to Cartesia, together with the name you gave the voice profile and the language you chose, to create the voice model; the voice model is stored on Cartesia's infrastructure and used to narrate your stories. For every narration with your cloned voice, the text to be narrated is also sent to Cartesia. Cartesia processes this data on our behalf as a data processor. For this reason, the processing of your voice data involves a **transfer of data abroad** and is based on your explicit consent.
- **Ready-Made Voices:** Narrations with ready-made narrator voices are produced with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. For this, only the text to be narrated is sent to Google; your voice recording is not sent.
- **Email Delivery:** We send service emails, such as the welcome email and the password reset email, through Resend, Inc. (United States of America). For this purpose, your email address and your name are passed to Resend. The password reset link is created by our authentication provider, Firebase (Google).
- **Story Translation and Tagging:** Google Gemini is used to translate the texts in our story catalogue into other languages, to split them into segments for narration and to tag them by language level and age group. Only the story texts in the catalogue are sent for this processing; your personal data and your voice recordings are not sent.
- **Push Notifications:** We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and the Apple Push Notification Service (APNS) to send push notifications.
- **Legal Obligations:** We may disclose your information if there is a legal obligation, a court order or an official request.

The servers of the service providers listed above may be located outside Türkiye. For this reason, data shared with these providers may also be transferred abroad.

## Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet and no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active or as long as it is needed to provide the Service to you.

- **Voice Samples and Voice Models:** Your raw voice recording, the processed copy prepared from it, your consent recording (if any) and your voice model are kept until you delete the relevant voice profile or your account. When you delete them, this data is deleted from our storage and from Cartesia. If the deletion on the provider's side cannot be completed because of a temporary error, our system retries the deletion automatically.
- **Deletion Records:** The record showing that a voice model was deleted (it contains no audio; it contains the user identifier, the name of the voice profile, the voice identifier at the provider and the date) is kept so that we can meet our legal obligations.
- **Store Records:** Records of the subscription notifications and verifications received from the app stores (they contain no audio and no payment details) are kept after your account is deleted, with their link to your account removed, to prevent the same transaction from being processed twice and for record-keeping.
- **Technical Logs:** Server technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting. These logs are not deleted separately when your account is deleted.

We may keep your information for as long as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. You can request the deletion of your data and close your account at any time.

## Explicit Consent and Your Rights

You can withdraw the explicit consent you gave for voice cloning at any time. To do so, it is enough to delete the relevant voice profile in the app. Deleting your account also deletes all your voice recordings and voice models. For your other rights under Article 11 of the KVKK (to request information, rectification, erasure, to object and similar rights), you can reach us at [info@sesly.io](mailto:info@sesly.io).

## Children's Privacy

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. The Service is designed for parents and other adults who care for children to play stories to children; children do not create accounts and do not record their voices. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you become aware that your child has given us personal information, please contact us.
