Protect your privacy and manage your data on Sesly. This page explains how to delete a voice profile, or your account and all the data that belongs to you.

## Deleting a Voice Profile

When you delete a voice profile you created with your own voice:

- Your raw voice recording, the processed copy prepared from it and your consent recording (if any) are deleted from our storage.
- Your voice model is deleted from our voice cloning provider, Cartesia.
- The sample audio and the story narrations made with this voice are deleted.
- The explicit consent you gave for this voice is withdrawn.

To delete a voice profile, select the voice in your voice list on the **My voice** tab of the app and use the delete option.

## How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all the data associated with it directly in the Sesly app. This action is **permanent** and cannot be undone.

1. Open the **Sesly** app on your device.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top of the screen to open the **Profile** page.
3. Tap **Delete account** at the bottom of the page.
4. Read which data will be deleted and tick the confirmation box.
5. Tap the **Delete my account** button and confirm your choice in the dialog that appears.

If you have a paid subscription, cancel it in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store subscription settings before you delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel your store subscription.

## What Data Is Deleted?

- **Personal Profile:** Your name, email address, profile photo and preferences.
- **Created Content:** All the story narrations and audio files you created.
- **Voice Recordings and Voice Models:** All your voice samples, their processed copies and your consent recordings are deleted from our storage; your voice models are deleted from Cartesia.
- **Other Account Data:** Your favorites, playlists, listening progress, translation requests, notifications, device notification identifiers and usage counters.
- **Subscription Records:** Your subscription and purchase records at Sesly are deleted. Your store subscription, however, is not cancelled; it continues to renew and to be charged by the relevant store until you cancel it.
- **Sign-In Account:** Your Firebase authentication account is deleted.

**Deletion at the provider:** Every voice model deletion request is carried out immediately by calling Cartesia's deletion interface, and its result is recorded. If a temporary error occurs, our system automatically retries the deletion several times; if it still cannot be completed, the request is flagged for manual follow-up.

**Records that are kept:** The record showing that the deletion was carried out (it contains no audio; it contains the user identifier, the name of the voice profile, the voice identifier at the provider and the date) is kept for our legal obligations. Records of the subscription notifications received from the app stores (they contain no audio and no payment details) are kept with their link to your account removed. Our servers' technical logs (they contain information such as the IP address and the account identifier) are not deleted separately when your account is deleted and are kept for a limited period.

## Manual Request

If you cannot access the app, you can send us an email at [info@sesly.io](mailto:info@sesly.io) to request the deletion of your account. For verification, please send the email from the email address registered to your account.
